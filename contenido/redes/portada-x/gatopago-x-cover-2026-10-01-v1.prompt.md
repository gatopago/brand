## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: final X / Twitter profile header for GatoPago, a wide panoramic 3:1 image, ideally 1500 x 500 px. Produce the actual banner artwork, not a mockup of a social profile.
Primary request: a beautiful, restrained, artistic brand cover with ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT. Sophisticated, warm, playful pixel-art editorial composition.
Input image 1: reference for the existing GatoPago seated orange tabby character. Preserve its recognizable feline design: very large triangular ears, coral-orange fur, dark red ear interiors and three forehead stripes, two square black eyes, small omega smile, thick almost-black stepped outline, compact seated feline body, ringed tail curled beside its feet. One cat only.
Backdrop and composition: expansive flat warm Milk cream #FFF8F0; approximately two-thirds generous uninterrupted negative space. Leave lower-left area empty because X profile avatar overlaps there. A quiet flowing horizontal journey made of a VERY FEW coral rectangular pixel steps rises from low left-middle toward the right, like abstract architectural stepping stones. Concentrate the visual rhythm across the middle-right. Place the seated cat small-to-medium near 76% of the width, fully visible, calmly sitting on the final coral block. The cat should occupy about 25% of the banner height, never become an enormous mascot poster.
Art direction: tasteful minimalist graphic art; clear geometric silhouettes, deliberate spacing, subtle asymmetry, an understated playful composition. The pixel path should feel artfully composed, not a loading indicator, not a row of many disconnected confetti squares. Limited elements, no decorative clutter. Clean two-dimensional aesthetic; hard stepped edges with consistent square units. A small restrained Cat Shadow #CF3433 offset on coral blocks adds the brand's flat printed depth. No realistic cast shadows. Cream remains the overwhelming dominant color.
Palette: Milk #FFF8F0, Cat Fire #F85239, Cat Shadow #CF3433, Ink #0B0B0F. Do not add purple, blue, gold or multicolor accents.
Constraints: no letters, no numbers, no wordmark, no typography, no caption, no watermarks. No banknotes, coins, dollar signs, cryptocurrency symbols, QR codes, UI cards, buttons, gradients, glow, 3D, photography, checkerboard transparency or illustrated browser frame. Do not crop ears, whiskers, paws or tail. Keep key artwork away from very top and bottom edges for header cropping. Opaque cream background edge to edge. A genuinely elegant, simple finished banner, not a busy game scene.
