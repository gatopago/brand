# GatoPago

**Last updated:** August 23, 2026  
**Status:** current English narrative  
**Scope:** messaging source for one-pagers, Notion pages, and pitches; it does not prove technical or commercial availability

**Your dollars already know how to move.**

GatoPago is a programmable onchain account for getting paid, paying, swapping, and putting part of your USDC to work without giving up control.

> Your money stays yours. GatoPago handles the route.

## The idea

Receiving USDC is only the beginning. The real problem comes next: deciding what stays ready for everyday use, what can grow, and how to use it without jumping between wallets, protocols, and networks.

GatoPago brings that cycle into one account:

1. **Receive:** get paid through links, QR codes, or transfers.
2. **Available:** keep USDC ready to pay, send, swap, or withdraw.
3. **Growing:** move the part you choose into Aave V3 in search of yield.
4. **Use:** make it Available again when needed.

## What you can try today

- Create an account with a passkey from the browser.
- Receive and send USDC.
- Create payment links and QR codes.
- Pay usernames.
- Swap between USDC and ETH with a visible quote and costs.
- Deposit and withdraw USDC through Aave V3 from the Growing experience.
- Review activity, receipts, and onchain transactions.
- Test payment intents, checkout, sandbox, and webhooks through the API.

The current public version is an **Alpha on Arbitrum Sepolia with test funds**. It is not mainnet.

## Control without hiding the fine print

Funds live in a smart account and every movement requires an active key. You sign in with a passkey, such as your fingerprint or face, without storing a seed phrase.

Assisted recovery can initiate a key replacement with a 48-hour delay. An active key can cancel it during that time.

GatoPago charges a **0% fee between its users**. A network, protocol, conversion, or external provider may have costs of its own; any known cost is shown before confirmation.

Growing uses Aave V3. The rate is variable and DeFi involves risk. GatoPago simplifies the flow while keeping the protocol, network, contract, and exit route visible.

## Who it starts with

The first wedge is people and small teams in Latin America that already receive or use USDC:

- freelancers and creators;
- contributors and remote teams;
- founders and developers;
- Web3 communities;
- grant and hackathon recipients;
- small agencies and digital businesses.

They already understand why they want digital dollars. GatoPago must prove it helps them receive those dollars, decide what to do with them, and use them again.

## What comes next

**GatoPago Card** is being validated as an early-access product. The card is not active, and provider, coverage, eligibility, costs, and verification requirements are not final.

GatoPago is also researching better money-in and money-out rails for Bolivia and Latin America. Regulated services may require verification depending on the provider and jurisdiction.

## For developers

The GatoPago API lets products create payment intents, open a checkout, and receive signed webhooks when an onchain payment is confirmed. It includes sandbox, retries, idempotency, and status queries.

The API is a distribution and learning channel for pilot integrations. GatoPago Personal remains the center of the brand.

## Short pitch

GatoPago is a programmable onchain account for receiving, using, and growing digital dollars without giving up control. It combines passkey-secured accounts, payment links and QR, guided DeFi, and a future card, plus an API for products that need to accept stablecoins.
