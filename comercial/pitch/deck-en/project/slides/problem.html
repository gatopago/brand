$294M in crypto.
In six months.

Source: Central Bank of Bolivia, via Bloomberg Línea (Jul 2025)

In the first half of 2025, Bolivians moved 294 million dollars through crypto. Six times more than a year before. Not out of hype. For three years, banks rationed dollars, and people found another way. The demand for digital dollars is real.
