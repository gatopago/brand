Here's how we grow. We don't win users one by one: companies bring them. Payment companies, fintechs and businesses in Bolivia already have the customers, the trust, and the way to move bolivianos in and out. We give them the self-custodial dollar account, ready to plug in. Their customers get a dollar account they own, inside a brand they already use. That's B2B2C.
